PUBLIC HOLIDAY & OBSERVED HOLIDAY POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. OBSERVED HOLIDAYS
Acme Corp observes the following paid holidays (US employees):
1. New Year's Day — January 1
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day — Third Monday in January
3. Presidents' Day — Third Monday in February
4. Memorial Day — Last Monday in May
5. Juneteenth — June 19
6. Independence Day — July 4
7. Labor Day — First Monday in September
8. Thanksgiving Day — Fourth Thursday in November
9. Day after Thanksgiving — Fourth Friday in November
10. Christmas Day — December 25

2. FLOATING HOLIDAYS
In addition to the 10 observed holidays above, each employee receives 2 floating holidays
per calendar year to use at their discretion. Floating holidays do not carry over.

3. INTERNATIONAL EMPLOYEES
Employees based outside the US observe their local statutory public holidays.
The People team provides each country's specific holiday schedule at the start of each year.

4. HOLIDAY ON WEEKEND
When a holiday falls on a Saturday, the preceding Friday is observed.
When it falls on a Sunday, the following Monday is observed.

5. HOLIDAY PAY
Employees who work on an observed holiday receive standard pay for the day plus
one additional day of PTO added to their balance.
