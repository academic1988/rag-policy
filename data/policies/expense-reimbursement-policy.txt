EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. PURPOSE
This policy ensures employees are fairly reimbursed for business-related expenses
incurred while performing their duties.

2. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
- Business travel (flights, trains, hotels, ground transport)
- Client meals and entertainment (with prior manager approval)
- Work-from-home internet subsidy: up to $50/month
- Professional development (books, courses, conferences) — see Learning & Development Policy
- Office supplies for home office (up to $100/year without prior approval)

3. INELIGIBLE EXPENSES
- Alcohol (unless explicitly pre-approved for a client event)
- Personal entertainment or leisure
- Traffic fines or parking violations
- Upgrades (e.g., business class airfare) unless a flight exceeds 6 hours

4. SUBMISSION PROCESS
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
Required documentation: itemised receipt for any single expense over $25.
Submit claims via the Concur expense system.

5. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
- Up to $500: direct manager approval
- $500–$2,000: department head approval
- Over $2,000: CFO approval required

6. REIMBURSEMENT TIMELINE
Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days via direct deposit
to the employee's bank account on file.

7. TRAVEL — ADVANCE BOOKING
Flights and accommodation must be booked at least 14 days in advance where possible
to ensure eligibility for reimbursement of standard rates.
