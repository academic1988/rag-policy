LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. LEARNING BUDGET
Each full-time employee receives an annual Learning & Development (L&D) budget:
- Individual Contributors: $1,500/year
- Managers and above: $2,500/year
Budgets reset on January 1 and do not roll over.

2. ELIGIBLE ACTIVITIES
- Online courses and certifications (e.g., Coursera, Pluralsight, AWS certifications)
- Professional conferences (registration + travel, subject to manager approval)
- Books and technical reference materials
- Coaching or mentoring programs
- Industry association memberships

3. INELIGIBLE EXPENSES
- Degree programs (handled separately under the Tuition Assistance Policy)
- Activities unrelated to the employee's current or planned future role

4. APPROVAL PROCESS
Submit L&D requests via the HR portal. Requests under $200 are auto-approved.
Requests over $200 require manager approval within 5 business days.

5. CONFERENCE ATTENDANCE
Employees presenting at conferences may receive additional budget with VP approval.
After attending a conference, employees are expected to share key learnings with
their team within 30 days (e.g., a short presentation or written summary).

6. TUITION ASSISTANCE
For degree programs, a separate Tuition Assistance Policy provides up to $5,250/year
(IRS tax-free limit). Eligibility requires 1+ year of tenure and manager + HR approval.
Employees must remain with the company for 1 year post-completion or repay assistance.
