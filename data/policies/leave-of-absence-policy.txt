LEAVE OF ABSENCE POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. TYPES OF LEAVE

A. FAMILY & MEDICAL LEAVE (FMLA)
Eligible employees (12+ months tenure, 1,250+ hours worked) may take up to 12 weeks of
unpaid, job-protected leave per year for qualifying family or medical reasons.
Qualifying reasons include: serious health condition, care for a family member,
or birth/adoption of a child.

B. PARENTAL LEAVE
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid leave at 100% base salary
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks paid leave at 100% base salary
Parental leave must commence within 12 months of birth, adoption, or foster placement.

C. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
- Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 days paid
- Extended family (grandparent, in-law): 3 days paid
- Close friend or colleague: 1 day paid (manager discretion)

D. JURY DUTY
Employees called for jury duty receive full pay for up to 15 business days.
Provide a copy of the summons to HR within 3 days of receipt.

E. MILITARY LEAVE
Employees are entitled to unpaid leave for military service as required by law (USERRA).
Benefits continuation is available for up to 24 months.

2. REQUESTING LEAVE
Submit a Leave of Absence request via the HR portal with at least 30 days' notice
where foreseeable. For emergencies, notify HR and your manager as soon as practicable.
