REMOTE WORK & HYBRID WORK POLICY
Effective Date: March 1, 2024

1. ELIGIBILITY
Remote work arrangements are available to full-time employees whose roles do not require
on-site physical presence. Eligibility is determined by the employee's manager and HR.

2. HYBRID SCHEDULE
The default hybrid schedule requires employees to be on-site a minimum of 2 days per week,
typically Tuesday and Thursday, unless otherwise agreed with their manager.

3. FULLY REMOTE
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and are reviewed annually.
Employees in fully remote roles must attend quarterly in-person team gatherings.

4. HOME OFFICE REQUIREMENTS
Employees must maintain a safe, distraction-free home workspace with:
- Reliable internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps download)
- Company-issued equipment (personal devices require BYOD approval)
- A dedicated workspace that allows for confidential calls

5. EQUIPMENT STIPEND
Employees receive a one-time home-office setup stipend of $500 upon approval of
a remote or hybrid arrangement. An annual refresh stipend of $200 is available
from Year 2 onwards.

6. CORE HOURS
All employees, regardless of location, must be available during core hours:
10:00 AM – 3:00 PM in their team's primary time zone.

7. PERFORMANCE EXPECTATIONS
Remote work is a privilege. Managers may revoke remote work arrangements if performance
or communication standards are not met, with 2 weeks' written notice.
