INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Effective Date: February 1, 2024

1. PURPOSE
To protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of company and customer data.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties accessing company systems.

3. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum 14 characters
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and a special character
- Passwords must not be reused within the last 12 cycles
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts

4. DEVICE SECURITY
- Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled (BitLocker / FileVault)
- Screen lock must activate after 5 minutes of inactivity
- Personal devices accessing company data must be enrolled in Mobile Device Management (MDM)
- Do not connect company devices to public Wi-Fi without using the company VPN

5. DATA CLASSIFICATION
- Public: marketing materials, published content
- Internal: general business documents (default classification)
- Confidential: customer data, financials, HR records — requires encrypted storage/transfer
- Restricted: source code, encryption keys — access strictly controlled

6. INCIDENT REPORTING
Security incidents (data breaches, phishing, lost devices) must be reported to the
Security team within 1 hour of discovery: security@acmecorp.com or call IT Help Desk.

7. ACCEPTABLE USE
Company systems are for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted but must not:
- Compromise system security or performance
- Violate any law or this policy

8. TRAINING
All employees must complete the annual security awareness training within 30 days of hire
and each January thereafter.
