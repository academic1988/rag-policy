CODE OF CONDUCT
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. INTRODUCTION
All employees, contractors, and representatives of Acme Corp are expected to uphold
the highest standards of professional conduct and ethics.

2. CORE PRINCIPLES
- Integrity: Be honest in all dealings with colleagues, clients, and partners.
- Respect: Treat all individuals with dignity regardless of background, identity, or role.
- Accountability: Take responsibility for your actions and decisions.
- Confidentiality: Protect company, client, and colleague information at all times.

3. PROHIBITED CONDUCT
The following behaviours are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate termination:
- Harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind
- Theft, fraud, or intentional misrepresentation
- Sharing confidential information without authorisation
- Falsifying company records or expense claims
- Conflicts of interest not disclosed to HR

4. REPORTING VIOLATIONS
Employees must report suspected violations to HR or via the anonymous ethics hotline:
ethics@acmecorp.com | 1-800-555-0199 (24/7).
Retaliation against anyone reporting in good faith is prohibited.

5. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
Employees must disclose potential conflicts of interest in writing to their manager and HR.
Common examples: outside employment, investments in competitors, personal relationships
with direct reports.

6. SOCIAL MEDIA
Do not share confidential or proprietary company information on social media.
When posting in a personal capacity, make clear views are your own, not Acme Corp's.
