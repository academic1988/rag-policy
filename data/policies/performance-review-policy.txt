PERFORMANCE REVIEW & FEEDBACK POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. REVIEW CYCLE
Acme Corp conducts formal performance reviews twice per year:
- Mid-Year Review: July (covers January–June performance)
- Annual Review: January (covers July–December + full-year summary)

2. RATING SCALE
Performance is rated on a 5-point scale:
5 — Exceptional: Consistently exceeds all expectations
4 — Exceeds Expectations: Regularly surpasses goals
3 — Meets Expectations: Achieves all core objectives
2 — Needs Improvement: Partially meets expectations; development plan required
1 — Unsatisfactory: Fails to meet expectations; may trigger a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

3. SELF-ASSESSMENT
Employees complete a self-assessment form at least 5 business days before their scheduled
review meeting. The form is submitted via the HR portal.

4. 360-DEGREE FEEDBACK
Managers may collect optional peer and cross-functional feedback for employees at
Senior level and above.

5. COMPENSATION IMPACT
Annual merit increases are directly tied to performance ratings:
- Rating 5: 6–10% salary increase
- Rating 4: 4–6%
- Rating 3: 2–4%
- Ratings 1–2: No increase; development plan initiated

6. PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN (PIP)
Employees rated 1 or 2 consecutively may be placed on a 90-day PIP.
Successful completion results in rating re-assessment. Failure may result in termination.
