PAID TIME OFF (PTO) POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. OVERVIEW
All full-time employees are eligible for Paid Time Off (PTO). PTO covers vacation, personal days,
and non-illness-related absences.

2. PTO ACCRUAL
- Years 0–2: 15 days per year (1.25 days/month)
- Years 3–5: 20 days per year (1.67 days/month)
- Years 6+:  25 days per year (2.08 days/month)
Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis.

3. CARRYOVER
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year.
Days in excess of 5 are forfeited on December 31.

4. REQUESTING PTO
Submit PTO requests via the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for requests of 3+ days.
For single-day requests, 2 business days' notice is sufficient when possible.
All requests are subject to manager approval.

5. PAYOUT ON TERMINATION
Accrued, unused PTO will be paid out upon voluntary resignation or termination,
up to a maximum of 10 days.

6. HOLIDAYS
PTO does not apply to the company's designated public holidays (see Holiday Policy).
