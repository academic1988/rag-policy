EMPLOYEE DATA PRIVACY POLICY
Effective Date: April 1, 2024

1. PURPOSE
This policy explains how Acme Corp collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data
related to employees and job applicants, in compliance with applicable privacy laws
(GDPR, CCPA, and others).

2. DATA WE COLLECT
- Contact information (name, address, email, phone)
- Employment history and qualifications
- Compensation and benefits data
- Performance and disciplinary records
- Health information where required (e.g., accommodation requests)
- Device usage logs for company-owned equipment

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
Employee data is used solely for legitimate employment and business purposes including:
payroll processing, benefits administration, compliance reporting, and performance management.

4. DATA RETENTION
- Active employee records: retained throughout employment + 7 years after termination
- Applicant data (not hired): retained for 2 years
- Payroll records: retained for 7 years per statutory requirements

5. YOUR RIGHTS
Employees have the right to:
- Access their personal data held by the company
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)
Submit requests to privacy@acmecorp.com.

6. DATA SHARING
Employee data is not sold. It may be shared with benefit providers, payroll processors,
and government agencies only as required for employment administration or legal compliance.

7. DATA BREACHES
Suspected breaches involving employee data must be reported to privacy@acmecorp.com
within 24 hours of discovery.
